About

A trading house, not a middleman.

We hold floor stock in Deira and consolidate containers out of Jebel Ali, so buyers can move from swatch to shipment without adding avoidable hands in the chain.

Most fabric in the region passes through three or four desks before it reaches the cutter's table. Elmira keeps the route shorter: pick the mill, qualify the run, ship it correctly, and put it on a roll in Dubai where buyers can inspect the hand before they commit.

We do not retail. We do not stitch. Our work is fabric in volume, with specifications, paperwork, and freight treated as seriously as the cloth itself.

Services

Beyond the roll: sourcing, finance, and freight.

Built for B2B buyers who need dependable fabric, clear terms, and fewer surprises between mill, port, and warehouse.

/ 01

Wholesale supply

Floor stock and forward orders against standing qualities. Same-week pickup from Deira or consolidated container movement from Jebel Ali.

/ 02

Custom sourcing

Send a swatch and a quantity. We qualify mills against price, hand, shrinkage, lead time, and repeat availability before quoting.

/ 03

Logistics and LC

FOB, CFR, and DAP options. Letter of credit, TT, and 30/60-day terms are available for established trade accounts.
